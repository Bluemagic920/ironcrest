Ironcrest Workforce Operations

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Ironcrest Workforce Operations ("Ironcrest") is a workforce management service provided by ProfitHarbor AI. This policy explains what information Ironcrest handles, why, and the choices you have. It covers company owners and the employees they invite.

Information we handle

  • Account details. Your sign-in email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to Ironcrest staff or to your employer.
  • Company details. Company name, time zone, workweek settings, worksite names, worksite coordinates and allowed radius, entered by the company owner.
  • Employee records. Name, job title, employment status, phone number and contact email, entered by the owner or by the employee.
  • Work records. Invitations, scheduled shifts, assigned jobs and their status, company announcements, and attendance entries (clock-in and clock-out times and the worksite).
  • Technical data. Basic request information such as IP address and browser type, used for security and rate limiting.

Location

When an employee presses Clock in or Clock out, Ironcrest asks the device for one fresh location reading and uses it to check whether the employee is inside the worksite boundary set by the employer. Ironcrest does not track location in the background or between those actions.

The coordinates from that reading are used for the check and are not saved in the attendance record. The record keeps the time, the worksite and the result. Device location can be inaccurate or altered, so a location check is not proof of physical presence.

Employees can refuse location permission in their browser, but clock-in and clock-out will not work without it. Employees who cannot share location should speak with their employer.

How information is used

  • To provide the service: sign-in, invitations, scheduling, jobs, announcements and attendance.
  • To show each company its own records. Owners see records for their company; employees see their own.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and investigate problems.
  • To manage subscriptions and billing for company owners.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Company owners can see the employee, schedule, job and attendance records for their own company. Employees can see their own profile, schedule, jobs, time records and their company's published announcements.

We use service providers to run Ironcrest, and they process information on our behalf: Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudflare (application hosting and security), Resend (invitation emails), GitHub Pages and jsDelivr (delivery of the website), and a payment processor for subscriptions. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Retention

Records are kept while the company's account is active, and afterwards for as long as needed to meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations. Employers may have their own legal duties to keep time records.

Your choices

Employees can update their phone number and contact email in My Profile. For other corrections, access or deletion requests about employment records, contact your employer first, since they control those records. You can also contact us using the details on the Support page. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law.

Security

Access is limited to verified members of each company, connections are encrypted, and sensitive actions are checked on our servers rather than trusted from the browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Ironcrest is a workplace tool and is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ProfitHarbor AI through the Support page.
