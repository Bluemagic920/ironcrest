Employees

Your employer manages your invitation, profile, worksite assignments, schedule and time records. Contact your company owner first if:

  • your invitation link has expired or does not work,
  • you cannot clock in or out at your worksite,
  • a shift or time record looks wrong.

Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the Employee Portal sign-in screen.

Sign in on the Ironcrest home page with your ProfitHarbor AI account. Use Forgot password? there if you cannot sign in.
